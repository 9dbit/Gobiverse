## 6. Casino & Slot Knowledge Standard

Knowledge content may cover:

- RTP, volatility, hit frequency, paytables, paylines, ways-to-win, RNG, house edge, probability, and variance.
- Wild, scatter, free spins, multipliers, cascades, feature buys, maximum wins, and jackpots.
- Table-game rules and mathematical differences.
- Game art, sound design, animation, narrative, UX, live-dealer production, certification, and testing.
- History, mythology, culture, providers, and game-development processes.

Every educational claim must distinguish:

1. **Game rules** — what the game formally does.
2. **Mathematics** — long-run theoretical behavior.
3. **Session outcome** — unpredictable individual results.
4. **Opinion** — editorial judgment that must be labelled as such.

RTP must never be described as a guaranteed return for a player or a session. Independent RNG outcomes must not be presented as predictable patterns.

### Prohibited claims

- “Guaranteed win,” “pasti menang,” or equivalent.
- “Pola gacor,” “jam gacor,” “anti-rungkad,” or “bocoran RTP” presented as factual winning methods.
- Guaranteed income or recovery of losses.
- Fabricated wins, fake testimonials, or manipulated balances.
- Instructions designed to bypass platform, legal, age, payment, or geographic restrictions.
- Covert redirects, cloaking, or disguised links.
- Encouraging chasing losses, borrowing, or playing with essential funds.

These terms may be discussed only in a clearly educational **Myth vs Math** context that corrects misinformation.

## 7. Editorial & Platform Safety

Before publication, confirm:

- Audience and jurisdiction are permitted.
- Age gating is applied when relevant.
- No minor-coded talent, language, or placement.
- Claims are factual, sourced, and not misleading.
- No promise of financial return.
- A responsible-play message is present when the content directly discusses real-money play.
- Links and calls to action comply with the destination platform's current rules.
- Licensed commercial promotion is kept separate from the general media/editorial layer.

If legal or platform eligibility is uncertain, publish only neutral educational or entertainment content and remove commercial calls to action until reviewed.

## 10. Content Workflow

1. **Insight:** audience question, trend, myth, or story opportunity.
2. **Brief:** objective, audience, format, hook, evidence, CTA, and risk classification.
3. **Fact check:** rules, numbers, sources, trademarks, and jurisdiction.
4. **Script:** one idea, immediate hook, clear payoff.
5. **Production:** human host, GOBI/B-88, motion graphics, AI-assisted visual, or combination.
6. **Editorial review:** brand voice, factual accuracy, IP, age, platform, and responsible-play check.
7. **Publish:** native caption, subtitles, thumbnail, and platform-safe CTA.
8. **Measure:** 24-hour and 7-day results.
9. **Learn:** continue, revise, or retire the format.

No content skips editorial review because it is trending.

## 11. Content Brief Minimum

- Content ID and owner.
- Pillar and recurring series.

- Objective and one-sentence message.
- Hook, body, payoff, and CTA.

- Age/jurisdiction/platform risk.

- Primary success metric.
- 24-hour and 7-day performance.

Use `templates/content-brief.md`.

## 12. Success Metrics

Primary organic metrics:

- Three-second hold rate.
- Average watch time and completion rate.
- Shares and saves per 1,000 views.
- Meaningful comments per 1,000 views.
- Returning viewers.
- Follower conversion per 1,000 profile visits.
- Branded search growth.
- Audience use of “GOBI Crew,” GOBI, B-88, and show names.
- UGC submissions and participation in quests.

Views alone do not define success.

Pilot targets are directional until a 30-day baseline exists. The first objective is to identify:

- Two repeatable high-retention formats.
- One strong knowledge format.
- One character/story format audiences request again.
- One community mechanic that creates repeat participation.

## 14. Definition of Done

A content item is done only when:

- The message is clear without its caption.
- Facts and claims are checked.
- Assets are original, licensed, or documented.
- Subtitles and mobile-safe framing are complete.
- Age, jurisdiction, platform, and responsible-play checks pass.
- Tracking data and success metrics are defined.
- The result is logged after publication.
