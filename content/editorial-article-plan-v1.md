## Terminology rule

“Tips bermain” means understanding rules, comparing variants, avoiding common mistakes, setting limits, and making decisions consistently. It does not mean guaranteed-win methods, recovery systems, manipulated RTP claims, or predictions of random outcomes.

Money management limits exposure; it cannot convert a negative-expectation game into guaranteed income.

## Standard article structure

1. **Direct answer:** answer the title in the first 80–120 words.
2. **Definition:** define the game, mechanic, or myth.
3. **How it works:** explain steps, rules, or mathematics.
4. **Example:** use transparent hypothetical units, never fabricated player results.
5. **Common misconception:** clearly separate myth from fact.
6. **Decision checklist:** rules, payout, probability, time limit, and budget limit.
7. **Responsible close:** remind readers that gambling is entertainment, not income.
8. **Sources:** cite current regulators, standards, rules, or reviewed game documentation.

## Editorial guardrails

- Default to 21+ where the jurisdiction or platform threshold is unclear.
- Educational portal only: no registration, deposit, bonus, affiliate, or operator redirect.
- Never promise wins, income, recovery, “anti-rungkad,” or predictive patterns.
- “Jam gacor” may appear only as a myth being tested and corrected.
- Do not publish fake RTP, live RTP, win-rate, player-balance, or testimonial data.
- Clearly distinguish formal game rules, long-run mathematics, individual session outcomes, and editorial opinion.
- Tips must never encourage chasing losses, borrowing, increasing stakes to recover, or using essential funds.
- Product-specific numbers require a reviewed information sheet and a recorded source date.
- Every article requires unique writing, named author/reviewer, verified sources, internal links, and human QA.
