# GOBI P005 — Near Miss Bukan Sinyal

**Series:** Myth Lab 04  
**Headline:** `NEAR MISS BUKAN SINYAL`  
**Format:** 9:16 vertical video  
**Runtime:** 34 seconds  
**Audience:** Adults 18+

## Core explanation

A near miss is a losing outcome that looks visually close to a winning arrangement. The feeling of “almost” can be memorable, but it is not progress toward a win and does not predict the next outcome. In a properly operating RNG-based game, the next request is resolved separately under the configured probabilities.

## Accuracy note

Do not describe near miss as increasing or decreasing the next outcome's probability. Do not imply that the game “owes” a win after repeated losses. Specific game probabilities require a reviewed game information source.

## Caption draft

Near miss bisa terasa sangat dekat dengan kemenangan. Namun secara hasil, near miss tetap merupakan outcome kalah yang sudah selesai—bukan progress menuju kemenangan berikutnya.

Tampilan “hampir” dapat menarik perhatian dan terasa lebih mudah diingat. Perasaan itu bukan sinyal, prediksi, atau alasan untuk mengejar hasil.
