## Knowledge locks

- RTP describes a theoretical long-run average across a very large number of outcomes.
- Volatility describes distribution and variation along the journey.
- Similar RTP does not imply a similar short-session experience or risk pattern.
- Neither concept predicts the next outcome or guarantees an individual session.
- Lower or higher volatility must never be presented as universally better.
