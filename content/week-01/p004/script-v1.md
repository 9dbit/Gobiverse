# GOBI P004 — RNG Tidak Punya Ingatan

**Series:** Myth Lab 03  
**Headline:** `RNG TIDAK PUNYA INGATAN`  
**Format:** 9:16 vertical video  
**Runtime:** 34 seconds  
**Audience:** Adults 18+

## Core explanation

In a properly operating RNG-based game, earlier results describe history but do not determine the next result. Each new request is resolved independently according to the system's configured probabilities. Independence does not mean every outcome is equally likely.

## Accuracy note

`Independent` does not mean every symbol or outcome has equal probability. Do not replace the approved wording with “semua hasil punya peluang sama”. Specific probabilities require a reviewed game information source.
