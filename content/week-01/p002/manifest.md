## Knowledge locks

- RNG: each generated result is independent; earlier outcomes do not determine the next one.
- RTP: a theoretical long-run average, not a promise for a session or individual player.
- Volatility: a description of outcome-distribution pattern and risk, not a predictor.
- Near miss and streaks: not signals for the following result.
- Responsible close: define time and spending limits before play and stop when the limit is reached.
