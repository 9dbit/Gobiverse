## Pilot Rules

- One primary vertical video per day.
- Publish natively to the approved adult-audience channels.
- Re-edit hooks and captions per platform; do not blindly repost.
- Every knowledge post requires sources and a fact check.
- Every real-money topic receives age, jurisdiction, platform, and responsible-play review.
- No guaranteed-win claims, fabricated outcomes, or youth targeting.
- Record 24-hour and 7-day results.

## Week 1 Production Batch

### GOBI-P001 — Brand Reveal

**Hook:** “Selamat datang di dunia yang tidak hanya dimainkan—tetapi juga dipelajari dan ditentukan olehmu.”

**Beats:**

1. Empty futuristic desert.
2. Portal opens.
3. GOBI and B-88 enter frame.
4. Rapid glimpses of Academy, Arena, Lab, and Cinema.
5. End card: “ENTER THE GOBIVERSE.”

**CTA:** “Follow dan pilih misi pertama.”

### GOBI-P002 — RTP Explained

**Hook:** “RTP 96% bukan berarti setiap Rp100 pasti kembali Rp96.”

**Core explanation:** RTP is a long-run theoretical percentage calculated over a very large number of plays. It does not predict or guarantee one player's session.

**CTA:** “Istilah apa yang ingin GOBI bedah berikutnya?”

### GOBI-P003 — First Quest

**Hook:** “Satu portal membawa GOBI ke Neon Arena. Satu lagi ke Lost Temple.”

**CTA:** “Tulis ARENA atau TEMPLE. Pilihan terbanyak menjadi episode berikutnya.”

### GOBI-P004 — RNG Myth

**Hook:** “Apakah ada jam tertentu yang membuat RNG lebih mudah ditebak?”

**Core explanation:** A properly functioning RNG does not become predictably favorable because of local time. A game-specific rule or scheduled tournament is different and must be documented.

**CTA:** “Kirim mitos berikutnya untuk Myth vs Math.”

### GOBI-P005 — Sound Design

**Hook:** “Mengapa kemenangan kecil bisa terdengar sangat besar?”

**Core explanation:** Layered sound, rising pitch, tempo, visual synchronization, and celebration length can intensify perceived reward.

**CTA:** “Mainkan dengan suara atau silent?”

### GOBI-P006 — Baccarat Basics

**Hook:** “Banker di baccarat bukan berarti bandar.”

**Core explanation:** Banker and Player are betting positions/hands. Explain the basic objective and avoid implying a guaranteed strategy.

**CTA:** “Berikutnya: blackjack atau roulette?”

### GOBI-P007 — Cinema Episode 1

**Hook:** “GOBI membuka portal yang dipilih Crew—tetapi B-88 mendeteksi sesuatu di dalamnya.”

**Ending:** Cliffhanger based on the Day 3 winning vote.

**CTA:** “Apa yang harus dipindai lebih dulu?”

## Scale Decision After Day 30

Scale a format only if it shows at least two of:

- Above-median completion rate.
- Above-median shares or saves.
- Repeat audience requests.
- Strong follower conversion.
- Sustainable production cost and turnaround.

Retire or redesign formats that repeatedly underperform and produce no distinctive brand memory.
