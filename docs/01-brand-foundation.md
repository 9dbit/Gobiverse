## Why People Should Follow

GOBI88 must answer at least one of these needs in every post:

1. **Teach me:** explain a game concept I misunderstood.
2. **Show me:** present a visual or technological idea I have not seen.
3. **Entertain me:** deliver humor, spectacle, or story.
4. **Let me decide:** give me a meaningful choice that changes the next post.
5. **Connect me:** introduce a person, creator, game, vehicle, or subculture.

A post that only says “register,” “claim,” or “play now” does not meet the organic brand standard.

## Character Brief v0.1

### GOBI

- Identity: original fictional Asian woman, age 23 and clearly adult.
- Role: gaming entertainer, streamer, host, tech presenter, and guide through the GOBIVERSE.
- Face lock: warm adult Asian features, almond dark-brown eyes, refined natural makeup, and softly defined warm nude-red lips.
- Hair lock: long deep chestnut-brown layered hair, soft full fringe, and face-framing bangs.
- Mark lock: two parallel signal-red diagonal bars on each cheek.
- Headset lock: navy/black metallic over-ear broadcast headset, red illuminated ring, and boom microphone.
- Outfit lock: fitted high-neck operator jacket with balanced pearl-white/deep-navy angular panels, signal-red piping, metallic shoulder structure, GOBI88 chest badge, and red 88 upper-arm identifier.
- Personality: intelligent, confident, competitive, lighthearted, calm under pressure, with dry humor.
- Weakness: occasionally overconfident; B-88 corrects the facts.
- Never: fox/animal form, childlike proportions, school styling, pink/magenta, sexualized framing, copied character, or copied game UI.

### B-88

- Role: drone, camera, navigator, and fact-checker.
- Shape: compact circular/dual-loop geometry referencing 88.
- Voice: precise, slightly literal.
- Story function: correct GOBI when opinion is mistaken for fact.

## Voice Examples

Good:

- “RTP menjelaskan perilaku teoritis jangka panjang—bukan hasil sesi malam ini.”
- “GOBI Crew, pilih portal berikutnya.”
- “Mitosnya populer. Matematikanya berkata lain.”
- “Kita bedah gamenya, bukan menjanjikan hasilnya.”

Avoid:

- “Pasti menang.”
- “Jam gacor hari ini.”
- “Deposit sekarang sebelum terlambat.”
- “Strategi ini tidak mungkin kalah.”
