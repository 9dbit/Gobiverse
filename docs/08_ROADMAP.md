# 08 — Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation slice

Outcome: validated product skeleton and one working search-to-utility-to-commerce journey using clearly labelled demo data.

Exit gate: all foundation acceptance criteria and validation checks pass.

## Phase 1 — Mobile Legends topical authority

- replace demo entities with 30 verified hero records;
- build patch-aware builds and counters;
- add author/reviewer workflow and revision history;
- publish only pages that pass the index quality contract;
- establish Search Console baseline and query tracking.

## Phase 2 — Tournament directory

- moderated organizer submissions;
- verified registration URLs and closing dates;
- online, game, and city discovery pages;
- expired/cancelled handling and archive policy;
- organizer trust and abuse-reporting workflow.

## Phase 3 — Gear content and affiliate commerce

- original test methodology and comparison templates;
- affiliate disclosures and click measurement;
- 10+ verified gear review pages;
- merchant/feed quality checks;
- no internal checkout yet.

## Phase 4 — Gobiverse ecommerce

Proceed only when product supply, margins, customer service, shipping, returns, tax, privacy, and payment compliance are approved.

- original Gobiverse merchandise and gaming accessories;
- inventory and order management;
- authorized payment gateway;
- Product/Offer structured data and Merchant Center feed;
- customer support and return workflow.

## Phase 5 — Community and game expansion

- controlled community voting with anti-abuse measures;
- team and organizer profiles;
- Roblox and Free Fire verified content clusters;
- expansion based on Search Console demand, not page-count targets.

## Decision rule

No phase begins because the calendar says so. It begins only after the previous phase passes validation and its evidence supports the next investment.
