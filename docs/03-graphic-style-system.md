## 1. Design Idea

GOBI88 should feel like a contemporary gaming-entertainment editorial brand rather than a stream of generic promotional posters.

The system combines:

- Premium cinematic character imagery.
- Oversized editorial typography.
- Asymmetric crops and intentional overlap.
- Characters breaking through frames and interfaces.
- White-space-led layouts.
- Thin data/UI structures.
- Subtle halftone, scanline, grain, and restrained glitch.
- A repeatable navy, white, and red identity.

Owner references are used only for composition mechanics and visual energy. Their people, brands, logos, text, colors, trademarks, and exact layouts must not be copied.

## 2. Four Graphic Modes

### A. Signal Cinema

Best for:

- Gaming reactions.
- Streaming moments.
- AI and technology.
- Data-led explainers.
- GOBI Cinema teasers.

Construction:

- Deep-navy cinematic field.
- GOBI in a medium or three-quarter hero crop.
- White key light and restrained red rim.
- Two to four translucent navy UI cards.
- Thin white data lines and small red status accents.
- One short condensed headline.

Baseline headline example: `PLAY SMARTER.`

### B. Type Strike

Best for:

- Myth vs Math.
- Strong facts.
- Poll results.
- Fast opinion and commentary.
- Provider or mechanic comparisons.

Construction:

- Dominant pearl-white field.
- Oversized cropped navy grotesk type.
- One or two signal-red blocks or rules.
- GOBI overlaps the headline.
- Subtle halftone or scanline texture.
- One message only.

Baseline headline example: `MYTH VS MATH`

### C. Breakout Frame

Best for:

- GOBI Quest.
- Brand reveal and content launches.
- Competitive gaming.
- Community calls to participate.
- Entertainment-first hooks.

Construction:

- GOBI steps, reaches, or gestures through a navy device or portal frame.
- Controlled low-angle or wide-angle perspective.
- One foreground hand or shoe may be larger, but anatomy must remain credible.
- Floating white/navy/red UI cards.
- Strong negative space for headline and platform UI.

Baseline headline example: `ENTER THE GOBIVERSE`

### D. Editorial Mask

Best for:

- Responsible-play messages.
- Thoughtful casino and slot knowledge.
- Quotes and community statements.
- Founder/editorial notes.
- Serious myth correction.

Construction:

- Dominant white field.
- Two or three vertical masking panels.
- Dramatic face crop; eyes and red cheek marks remain readable.
- Oversized navy quote marks.
- One signal-red vertical rule.
- Short headline plus optional controlled body copy added in compositing.

Baseline headline example: `PAUSE. CHECK. PLAY.`

## 3. Official Color Use

| Role | Working color | Typical share |
|---|---|---:|
| Pearl White | `#F2F4F5` | 50–60% |
| Deep Navy | `#071B2E` | 25–35% |
| Signal Red | `#D92720` | 10–15% |
| Minor Graphite | `#242A31` | 0–5% |
| Studio Black Navy | `#030A12` | Mode-dependent |

Absolute prohibition:

- Pink and hot pink.
- Magenta, purple, and lavender.
- Cyan, turquoise, and bright royal blue.
- Orange, yellow, gold, and green as campaign accents.

Neutral skin, brown hair, natural eyes, and realistic material colors are permitted.

## 4. Typography

Working type stack:

- **Hero headline:** Anton, Archivo Black, or a comparable heavy grotesk.
- **Condensed hook:** Bebas Neue, Roboto Condensed Bold, or a comparable condensed sans.
- **Body and UI:** Inter or Archivo.
- **Optional editorial accent:** a restrained serif or handwritten accent may appear once, but never compete with the main headline.

Rules:

- Headline is normally 2–6 words.
- Use sentence case or uppercase deliberately; never mix randomly.
- Maximum two font families per post.
- Maximum three type sizes with clear hierarchy.
- Allow type to crop at the canvas edge only when the core phrase remains understandable.
- Generative output is a visual draft. Final headlines, logo, body copy, disclaimers, and numbers must be applied as controlled typography during compositing.
- Never publish pseudo-text.

## 5. Grid and Cropping

Standard canvases:

| Format | Use | Minimum safe area |
|---|---|---|
| 9:16 | Reels, Shorts, TikTok | 6% sides; 12% top and bottom |
| 4:5 | Instagram and Facebook feed | 7% all sides |
| 1:1 | Feed, thumbnail, community post | 8% all sides |
| 16:9 | YouTube, web, landscape key visual | 6% all sides |

Character rules:

- Face or eyes must be readable at mobile thumbnail size.
- Do not crop both eyes, cheek marks, and headset simultaneously.
- Keep at least one complete brand anchor visible: face, chest plate, or arm `88`.
- Dynamic foreshortening is allowed in Breakout Frame only.
- Hands and shoes may enter foreground, but must not hide the face or headline.

## 6. Logo Use

- Use the supplied official GOBI88 logo without redesign.
- Preferred positions: top-left or bottom-right.
- Use one logo per frame.
- Maintain clear space equal to at least half the logo height.
- Do not rotate, stretch, outline again, recolor, or place on a visually noisy field.
- For small character chest plates, a blank navy plate is acceptable in generated imagery; add the official mark during compositing.

## 7. Texture and Effects

Permitted:

- Film grain at restrained strength.
- Halftone in selected background areas.
- Fine scanlines.
- One to three controlled glitch strips.
- Thin UI grids, rules, brackets, and status dots.
- Soft atmospheric particles in Signal Cinema.
- Clean device or portal frames in Breakout Frame.

Avoid:

- Heavy chromatic aberration.
- Random RGB glitch colors.
- Excessive bloom.
- Overlapping UI that obscures the face.
- Decorative effects with no information hierarchy.
- Generic casino gold, fire, money rain, or jackpot effects.

## 8. Content Mapping

| Content | Primary mode | Secondary mode |
|---|---|---|
| Gaming and streaming | Signal Cinema | Breakout Frame |
| AI and technology | Signal Cinema | Type Strike |
| Casino/slot mechanics | Type Strike | Editorial Mask |
| Myth vs Math | Type Strike | Signal Cinema |
| Responsible play | Editorial Mask | Type Strike |
| Community quest/poll | Breakout Frame | Type Strike |
| GOBI Cinema | Signal Cinema | Breakout Frame |
| Brand or season launch | Breakout Frame | Signal Cinema |

Casino and slot knowledge remains editorial for adults 21+. No visual may imply guaranteed wins, control over random outcomes, money recovery, or commercial access before the compliance gate is approved.

## 9. Motion Translation

For short-form video:

1. **0.0–0.7 s:** oversized headline or face reveal.
2. **0.7–2.0 s:** GOBI movement or frame break.
3. **2.0–6.0 s:** one explanation, reaction, or comparison.
4. **6.0–9.0 s:** data card, poll, or myth correction.
5. **Final 1.0 s:** logo plus one community CTA.

Motion rules:

- Type may slide, crop, scale, or reveal through masks.
- UI cards enter with disciplined horizontal or depth motion.
- Red is the action signal; white is information; navy is structure.
- Glitch is a transition accent, never a constant effect.
- Keep subtitle safe areas clear.
