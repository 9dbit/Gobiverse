1. Tall white/navy split collar with narrow red edge.
2. Pearl-white and deep-navy panels remain balanced; never white-only or bright-blue.
3. Angular seams and red piping create the operator silhouette.
4. Gunmetal shoulder structure reads as functional broadcast/tech apparel.
5. Chest badge and sleeve 88 remain legible when framing permits.
6. Hair may overlap the jacket but must not hide both cheek marks or the complete face.

## Automatic rejection

Reject any output with:

- a different face identity, lighter/blonde/black-blue hair, short hair, or missing fringe;
- three cheek triangles, one cheek mark, pink cheek paint, or unequal marks;
- pink/magenta/purple garment, makeup, glow, or UI;
- white-only suit, bright-blue bodysuit, generic esports jersey, or superhero armor;
- bunny emblem, D.Va/Overwatch cues, celebrity likeness, copied game UI, or copied logo;
- childlike proportions, sexualized styling, money rain, jackpot celebration, or guaranteed-win framing.
