## Source-use rules

- Prefer regulators, standards bodies, published rules, and reviewed research.
- A rule for one jurisdiction or game variant must not be generalized to every game.
- Record source title, URL, jurisdiction, version/update date, and access date.
- Product-specific RTP, payout, or feature data must come from the current official game information sheet.
- Screenshots, search snippets, AI summaries, and affiliate reviews are not final evidence.
- If two sources conflict, pause publication and resolve the rule/version difference.
