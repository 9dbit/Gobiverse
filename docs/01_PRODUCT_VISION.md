# 01 — Product Vision

## Product thesis

Gobiverse wins through **utility before commerce**. Search traffic should arrive because the platform solves a gamer’s task—choosing a hero, understanding a patch, finding a counter, discovering a tournament, or selecting suitable gear—not because it publishes generic news at scale.

Commerce is a relevant extension of the same audience journey, not a separate general marketplace.

## Target audience

Primary:

- Indonesian Mobile Legends players who want current, practical guidance;
- amateur teams, students, communities, and organizers looking for tournaments;
- mobile and PC gamers comparing affordable gaming gear.

Secondary, after topical authority is established:

- Roblox and Free Fire players looking for verified guides and codes;
- esports fans following schedules, standings, and event information.

## Core jobs to be done

1. “Help me choose the best counter for the enemy draft.”
2. “Show me a build that matches the current patch and my role.”
3. “Help me find a legitimate tournament I can still join.”
4. “Help me choose gaming gear based on real use, budget, and device.”
5. “Show me when information was verified and where it came from.”

## Differentiation

- interactive tools instead of listicles only;
- Indonesian-first explanations and filters;
- patch-aware, time-stamped, source-backed data;
- moderated tournament listings;
- first-hand gear testing and clear affiliate disclosure;
- one coherent gaming ecosystem across content, community, and commerce.

## Business model

Phased monetization:

1. contextual advertising after sufficient quality traffic;
2. disclosed affiliate links for legitimate gaming products;
3. sponsored tournament placements with clear labels;
4. original Gobiverse-branded gear;
5. ecommerce checkout only after supply, support, returns, and compliance are ready.

## Non-goals

Gobiverse is not:

- an online casino, betting, or real-money wagering platform;
- a generic news farm;
- a general ecommerce marketplace;
- a marketplace for game accounts, cheats, exploits, or unauthorized digital goods;
- an unofficial copy of game publishers’ databases or artwork;
- a site that fabricates “active” codes, win rates, reviews, or event availability.

## Success principles

- usefulness over page count;
- verified freshness over “latest” claims;
- mobile speed over visual excess;
- trust over aggressive conversion;
- original tools and experience over rewritten commodity content.
