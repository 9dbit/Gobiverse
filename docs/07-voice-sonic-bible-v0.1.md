## 1. Voice positioning

GOBI sounds like a clever young adult tech-entertainment host: intelligent, direct, curious, and lightly playful. She explains systems without sounding like a lecturer, salesperson, casino host, or childish game character.

**Voice age:** 23–27  
**Language:** natural Indonesian with controlled English technical terms  
**Accent:** neutral urban Indonesian / light Jakarta, never exaggerated  
**Pitch:** medium to medium-high, adult  
**Pace:** 150–160 words per minute; slow to 135–145 for definitions  
**Energy:** 7/10 hook, 6/10 explanation, 5/10 responsible close  
**Warmth:** 6/10  
**Confidence:** 8/10  
**Playfulness:** 4/10  
**Authority:** evidence-led, never absolute when evidence is limited

## 2. GOBI delivery pattern

1. **Hook:** short, confident, slight smile; emphasize the myth or question.
2. **Reset:** half-beat pause before the factual explanation.
3. **Explain:** clean phrasing, one idea per sentence, technical term followed by plain language.
4. **Fact lock:** lower pitch slightly on the key conclusion.
5. **Close:** calm and non-judgmental; responsible-play cues must not sound punitive.

### Signature language

- Primary verbal signature: `Data dulu. Baru percaya.`
- Myth correction: `Kedengarannya masuk akal. Datanya bilang berbeda.`
- B88 handoff: `B-delapan-delapan, tampilkan datanya.`
- Community question: `Mitos apa yang kita uji berikutnya?`

### Avoid

- Seductive, breathy, childish, squeaky, overly anime, or casino-host delivery.
- Shouting, fake excitement, jackpot reactions, urgency, FOMO, or loss-recovery language.
- Claiming certainty about random future outcomes.
- Overusing slang that makes knowledge content expire quickly.

## 4. B88 voice identity

B88 is a compact analytical companion, not a second presenter.

**Timbre:** clean synthetic-neutral with a warm digital edge  
**Pitch:** neutral-medium  
**Pace:** 165–180 words per minute  
**Sentence length:** normally 2–8 words  
**Emotion:** subtle confirmation, caution, and dry wit  
**Processing:** light formant texture and narrow stereo halo; speech remains fully intelligible

Approved examples:

- `Data terverifikasi.`
- `Tidak ada pola prediktif.`
- `Korelasi bukan kepastian.`
- `Batas tercapai. Saatnya berhenti.`
- `Catatan: itu mitos.`

## 5. Sonic identity

### Core cue kit

| Cue | Duration | Function | Sound |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Portal wake | 1.2 s | Brand opening | low metal rise, blue-cyan air, signal-red lock |
| GOBI sting | 1.8 s | Logo mnemonic | three-note ascending synth + short metal resolve |
| B88 chirp | 0.25 s | Companion entry | dual clean digital pulse |
| Data scan | 0.4 s | Fact display | narrow upward scan with soft glass tick |
| Myth busted | 0.55 s | Correction moment | muted red impact followed by clean blue resolve |
| Knowledge lock | 0.7 s | Key fact | low satin-metal click + short sub pulse |
| Responsible close | 1.0 s | Limit cue | warm descending tone, no alarm feeling |

### Music system

- Tempo range: 105–118 BPM.
- Tonal center: minor/modal with an optimistic resolved ending.
- Materials: restrained electronic pulse, textured bass, glass percussion, subtle metal impacts.
- Music stays below the voice and never imitates a casino machine.
- Prohibited: coin sounds, slot bells, jackpot fanfare, frantic ticking, win sirens, copyrighted game cues.

## 6. Mix and export targets

- Integrated loudness: approximately `-14 LUFS` for social masters.
- True peak ceiling: `-1 dBTP`.
- Voice remains clearly forward on phone speakers.
- Duck music 3–5 dB under definitions and responsible-play lines.
- B88 processing must not reduce consonant clarity.
- Deliver WAV 48 kHz / 24-bit stems plus AAC in final platform masters.

## 7. Approval test

Before final casting, test the same 12–15 second passage in three deliveries:

1. **Recommended:** intelligent tech host.
2. More cinematic and controlled.
3. More playful and community-led.

Approve based on adult credibility, clarity on technical terms, recognizability, and comfort over repeated daily posts—not novelty alone.
