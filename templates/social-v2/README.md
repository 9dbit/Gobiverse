## Active color behavior

- Deep navy/black: 65–75% of the visual.
- Pearl white and signal red: permanent brand anchors.
- Electric blue, cobalt, cyan, teal, amber, and orange: secondary luminous accents.
- Color should appear as light, glass refraction, gradient halo, indicator, or emissive seam.
- No pink, hot pink, magenta, pastel candy palette, or flat rainbow background.

## Layer contract

Keep the named groups `BACKGROUND`, `METAL_FRAME`, `SUBJECT`, `HEADLINE`, `PILL_UI`, `LOGO`, and `GUIDES`.
