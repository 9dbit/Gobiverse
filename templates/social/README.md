## Layer contract

Every master contains named groups for `BACKGROUND`, `HEADLINE`, `SUBJECT`, `UI`, `LOGO`, and `GUIDES`. Keep those names when importing into Figma, Illustrator, Affinity, or another SVG-aware editor.

- Replace the `SUBJECT IMAGE / MASK` placeholder with an approved canonical GOBI render.
- Replace sample headline text with controlled typography; never publish generated pseudo-text.
- Keep the supplied official wordmark linked or relink it from `assets/brand/gobi88-logo-official.png` (production-friendly PNG derived losslessly from the supplied WebP).
- Hide or delete the `GUIDES` group before export.
- Red is the action signal, white is information, and navy is structure.
- Do not introduce pink, magenta, purple, cyan, orange, yellow, gold, or green campaign accents.

## Export checklist

1. Confirm one message only.
2. Check face and headline at mobile-thumbnail size.
3. Keep logo clear space at least half its displayed height.
4. Remove guides and placeholder labels.
5. Add platform-safe subtitles/footers in the controlled edit.
6. Export sRGB PNG/WebP and retain the editable SVG master.
