# GOBI Voice Casting Test Pack v1

**Purpose:** Generate three directly comparable GOBI voice samples before final casting or TTS selection.  
**Language:** Indonesian  
**Target duration:** 12–16 seconds per sample  
**Required format:** WAV, mono, 48 kHz, 24-bit; no music, ambience, reverb, or character effects

## Identical test passage

> Mesin ini sedang panas? Kedengarannya masuk akal. Tapi data bilang berbeda. Ar-en-ji membuat setiap hasil berdiri sendiri. Data dulu. Baru percaya.

All candidates must record exactly the same words. Do not rewrite, add greetings, or change pronunciation.

## Candidate A — Intelligent Tech Host (recommended)

- Adult Indonesian female voice, perceived age 23–27.
- Neutral urban Indonesian / light Jakarta accent.
- Medium to medium-high pitch; clear adult tone.
- Pace around 155 words per minute.
- Confident and curious with a restrained smile.
- Start the question energetically, pause briefly before `Tapi`, lower the pitch on `berbeda`, and finish the signature with two deliberate beats.
- No breathy, seductive, anime, advertising, or casino-host delivery.

Suggested generic synthesis controls:

- Stability/consistency: medium-high.
- Expressiveness/style: medium.
- Similarity/voice fidelity: high.
- Speed: 1.00×.
- Pitch shift: neutral.

## Blind review procedure

1. Normalize all samples to equal perceived loudness; do not master them differently.
2. Rename temporary review copies `A.wav`, `B.wav`, and `C.wav`.
3. Listen once on phone speaker and once on headphones.
4. Score without seeing provider, actor, or model name.
5. Reject any sample that fails an automatic disqualifier.
6. Select one primary voice and one backup.

## Automatic disqualifiers

- Sounds under 21, childish, or anime-coded.
- Sounds seductive, breathy, or sexualized.
- Sounds like a casino host, hard-selling ad, or jackpot celebration.
- Mispronounces GOBI terms or Indonesian consonants.
- Cannot remain intelligible on a phone speaker.
- Voice identity changes significantly between sentences.
- Cannot reproduce the same tone across P001–P003.
